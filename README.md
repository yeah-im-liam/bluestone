# Bluestone: Over-Powered

Blue redstone that keeps to itself.

Inspired by [ajjxf's bluestone mod](https://modrinth.com/mod/bluestone_ajjxf).

## Features

- Bluestone dust, torches, repeaters, comparators, the Block of Bluestone, bluestone ores and bluestone powered, detector and activator rails. They work exactly like their redstone versions.
- Bluestone and redstone ignore each other: redstone dust, torches, repeaters, comparators, blocks and rails never pick up a bluestone signal, and the bluestone versions never pick up a redstone signal. Dust doesn't even bend towards the other kind. Run two circuits right next to each other without them mixing.
- Everything else works with both: levers, buttons, pressure plates, observers and other switches can start either signal, and both can power pistons, doors, lamps, TNT, dispensers and so on.
- Bluestone works underwater. Dust, torches, repeaters and comparators can be waterlogged, and flowing water doesn't wash them away. Purplestone dust, purplestone torches and the converter can't: like redstone, water washes them away.
- Anything powered (redstone, bluestone, purplestone or greenstone) gives off bubbles as well as its glow when it is underwater.
- Bluestone ore generates at the same heights as redstone ore. It is much more common in oceans, rivers and swamps, and a vein that touches water is four times as likely to appear as one that doesn't.
- Bluestone dust, purplestone dust and greenstone paste are armor trim materials, with their own blue, purple and green trim colours.
- Purplestone dust, purplestone torches and the Block of Purplestone work with both: redstone and bluestone power them, and redstone and bluestone dust, torches, repeaters, comparators and rails all pick up their signal at full strength. Mixing costs strength the other way: purplestone dust takes a redstone or bluestone signal at half strength, rounded down, so redstone dust at 10 gives it 5. Levers, buttons and other plain switches still give it full power. The converter changes colour without losing any strength.
- The converter is a repeater that turns a redstone signal into a bluestone one, or the other way round. Right-click it to swap the direction. Purplestone powers it in either direction. Like a repeater, a repeater or comparator (of either kind) pointing into its side locks it.
- Greenstone paste is redstone dust that sticks to floors, walls and ceilings. Unlike dust, it doesn't climb blocks by itself: you place it one face at a time, like glow lichen, and a single space can have paste on several of its faces. Each face looks and connects like dust, but only along its own surface: it joins paste next to it on the same surface, around the edge of a block, and on the other faces of its own space, and bends towards parts sitting on the same surface. Breaking paste takes off only the face you are looking at, and each face has its own outline, shaped like dust's. Right-click a lone piece to switch it between a cross and a dot, like dust. It loses one level of strength per block, around corners and up walls too. Greenstone torches, the Block of Greenstone, and greenstone repeaters and comparators go with it; the repeaters and comparators work on walls and ceilings as well, pointing any way along them. Only a greenstone repeater restores the signal. Redstone and bluestone ignore greenstone, and greenstone meets purplestone only through the wall converter.
- The wall converter is a full block that joins purplestone dust and greenstone paste on any side, both ways, passing on whatever strength it gets. It is not a repeater.
- Sticky rails stick to floors, walls and ceilings, and carts can go from floor to wall to ceiling. A cart climbs a wall on its own momentum, slowing as it goes, and rolls back down if it runs out; on a ceiling it needs some speed to stay on. There are sticky powered, detector and activator rails too, in a redstone and a bluestone kind, powered by their own colour, and they work on walls and ceilings as well: a powered one can push a cart up a wall. Rails on walls and ceilings turn to join the track around them, curve (plain sticky rails, like ordinary rails) and bend round inside corners, and carts on them are drawn turned to match, and floor rails, ordinary or sticky, join a wall rail running up from them (sloping up to meet it, the way rails go up a block) or down the side of the block they sit on.
- Chain rails, in a redstone and a bluestone kind, move carts at a fixed speed the way they were placed while powered by their colour, whatever speed the carts come in with, so carts can't roll back. Unpowered, they hold carts still. Sticky chain rails do the same on walls and ceilings, so they carry carts straight up a wall or along a ceiling.
- Novice clerics sell 2 bluestone dust instead of 2 redstone dust a quarter of the time.
- Programmer Art textures are included.

## Crafting

Same recipes as the redstone versions, with bluestone dust and bluestone torches in place of redstone dust and redstone torches.

- Purplestone dust: 1 redstone dust and 1 bluestone dust make 2.
- Purplestone torch: purplestone dust on a stick.
- Block of Purplestone: 9 purplestone dust, and back into 9 dust.
- Converter: a redstone torch, purplestone dust and a bluestone torch on top of three stone.
- Greenstone paste: 1 purplestone dust and 1 slimeball make 2, or 1 redstone dust, 1 bluestone dust and 2 slimeballs make 4.
- Greenstone torches, blocks, repeaters and comparators: the redstone recipes with greenstone paste and greenstone torches.
- Wall converter: purplestone dust, greenstone paste and stone.
- Chain rails: like the powered rail, with iron ingots, an iron chain and redstone dust (bluestone dust for the bluestone kind); makes 6.
- Sticky rails of every kind: 8 of the rail around a slimeball.

## Compatibility

- Minecraft: Java Edition 26.1 to 26.3
- Loaders: Fabric (with Fabric API) and NeoForge
- Environment: client and server

On NeoForge, turn on the "Bluestone: Over-Powered Programmer Art" resource pack together with the Programmer Art pack. Fabric switches the textures over by itself.
